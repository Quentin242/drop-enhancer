# Audio credits and permission

**daanbom / [Custom Sounds](https://github.com/daanbom/Custom-Drop-Sounds)** supplies the Common, Uncommon, Rare, Very rare and new collection-log sounds. On 2026-09-14, maintainer maiz confirmed permission from the Custom Sounds author to use these sounds in Drop Enhancer and publish this repository.

The **pet sound** remains the previously selected UniversalAudioSFX recording, `RewardsAndLootsSoundEffects/FindEpicItem02/SFX_FindEpicItemNoWhooshMusic01v1.wav`, under the author permission reported by maiz on 2026-09-14. The retained file is unchanged. The `botssouls-pet.wav` filename is a compatibility name, not an authorship credit.

Audio ownership and permission are separate from the BSD-2-Clause source-code license. These notices record permission for this plugin; they grant no standalone audio reuse rights. If permission changes, replace or remove the affected recordings from future distributions.

Exact source revision, paths, SHA-256 hashes and the Uncommon recording's PCM format conversion are included in `META-INF/sound-provenance.json`. The other Custom Sounds recordings are copied without audio edits. Old default names resolve to the new bundled recordings; user-imported local WAVs retain precedence.
